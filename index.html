About the Workshop

Important information

Please read everything before you register

WebCraft started in 2024 as a Saturday lab for students who were shipping project websites that worked and still felt tiring to use. The 2026 workshop keeps that aim. You will not collect a stack of new frameworks. You will practise a few decisions that change how a page feels: what the page is about, what the eye should meet first, which action matters, how type and spacing show that two things belong together, and what should happen after someone clicks. The notes below cover the day itself, the room, the fee, the certificate, what to bring, how refunds work, where to park, and how we expect people to behave in the hall. Read them once before you fill the form so the desk is not repeating them at 8:45.

What WebCraft is

A one-day practice session in looking at a website and making it easier to use. You already know how to build. This workshop is about deciding what a visitor should notice first, what can wait, and what should be taken off the page.

What You'll Learn

Six themes. All of them matter. None of them is optional.

The day is one workshop, not six courses. The cards are here so you can see the ground we will cover. You do not need to master a topic before you arrive.

●Module 1

React

45 minutes · also used all afternoon

You will open a component and trace what a visitor actually sees. Props, a bit of state, and a list are enough. We use them to talk about why a section is hard to change, not to start a new app.

  • Reading markup without rewriting it
  • Why six actions in one card tire people
  • Keeping a shared block when the styles disagree

Next.js

Reference only

The sample site is one page of sections. You will see how a link in the bar and a link in the hero can point at the same form, and why that still fails if the form does not look like the next step.

UI/UX

The whole morning

Hierarchy, grouping, and the path from the first screen to a completed registration. You will practise saying what the page is for in one sentence, then checking whether the page says the same thing.

  • What should be seen first
  • What a person is trying to finish
  • What you can remove without losing the day

Responsive Design

2:00 PM block

A layout that feels fine on a laptop can fall apart when the window gets narrower. You will try the page at a phone width and fix the places that should stack, wrap, or simply have less padding.

  • Columns that never stack
  • Type that becomes hard to read
  • Rows that force sideways scrolling
Open
◐Woven through

Accessibility

Not a separate hour

Contrast, names on controls, labels that say what to type, and focus that a keyboard user can see. These are part of the same critique, not a checklist you bolt on after the colours are chosen.

  • Text you can actually read
  • Controls with plain names
  • Errors that are not colour alone

⚡Short note

Performance

10 minutes inside fundamentals

We will not tune a bundle. We will notice when a page feels heavy because every block shouts, every card has a badge, and the photograph is larger than the task. Perceived effort counts here too.

  • Large images
  • Too many competing blocks
  • A first screen that takes work to parse

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers are below. Please read all of them before you write to the team, because the same questions come in every week and the organising group is small.

Who can register?

Students of any college who have built at least one website with HTML and CSS. A few seats are usually taken by students from outside Bengaluru. Working professionals can write to us, but the examples assume a student day.

Is there a fee?

Yes. ₹499 at the desk on the morning, by cash or UPI. That covers lunch and the certificate. The form keeps a place in the queue only until the hall reaches 40 paid seats. If we are full, we email you.

Do I need a laptop?

Yes. Bring it charged, with a current browser. A tablet is not enough for the afternoon challenge. You may borrow a laptop, but each person submits their own screenshot.

Will I get a certificate?

You will if you stay through the project challenge and submit the screenshot before 4:00 PM. Leaving at lunch means we cannot issue it. The PDF is sent within three weeks to the email on this form.

What should I install beforehand?

A current browser. Extra tools are useful and we will not spend the morning installing them. If setup fails, you can still do the critique half of the day. Please do not arrive expecting the desk to repair a laptop.

Can I get a refund?

Email webcraft@aether.edu.in by 17 October and we will refund the desk fee. After that we can transfer the seat to another student once. Write their name in the email. We do not refund a missed morning.

Is lunch included?

Yes. It is vegetarian, with an egg option if you say so when you pay at the desk. Tea is at 11:15 and 15:15. The cafeteria is open if you want something else, and outside food is better kept out of the hall.
